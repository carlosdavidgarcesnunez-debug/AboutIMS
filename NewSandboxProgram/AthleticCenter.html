Athletic Center


Information

23,000 Square Foot Facility Our new Athletic Center includes two basketball courts (also used for volleyball and tennis), a fitness center, training rooms, four squash courts and a viewing gallery. Centennial Field Thanks to the generosity of Kathleen and Lou Hager (Whit Hager ’21) this synthetic turf field is used our boys and girls soccer and lacrosse teams, providing premier competition space for two of our signature programs and is especially helpful during the wet shoulder seasons. Commitment to Sustainability Much like our net-zero Outdoor Working Lab (OWL), our Athletic Center is designed with sustainability in mind. With geothermal wells and solar panels, this building is energy efficient. The Importance of Community In addition to the Athletic Center’s physical attributes, the ability for IMS to hold community gatherings year-round is critical to the healthy growth and development of our community.
